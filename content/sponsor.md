Rust Trends reaches Rust developers, embedded/IoT engineers, and CTOs who read every issue for
signal, not noise. If you're hiring Rust engineers, building developer tooling, or selling
infrastructure that Rust teams use, this is a direct line to them.

## Audience

- **~1,500 subscribers** on the email list — Rust developers, enthusiasts, and CTOs
- **~4,500 followers** on <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/rust-trends" target="_blank">LinkedIn</a>, where every edition is also distributed
- **6–7% click-through rate** on newsletter links
- Every edition also gets submitted to <a href="https://this-week-in-rust.org/" target="_blank">This Week in Rust</a> and syndicated via RSS, and stays live permanently on rust-trends.com
- Biweekly cadence — one focused touchpoint every two weeks, not lost in a daily flood

## Why sponsor Rust Trends

- **Targeted, not broad.** Every subscriber opted in for Rust specifically — no generic dev-newsletter spray.
- **Permanent placement.** Editions live forever at rust-trends.com/newsletter/, so your mention keeps getting found via search long after send day.
- **Small enough to matter.** Your sponsorship funds an independent newsletter directly — no ad network markup.
